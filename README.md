# SellSnap

Snap a photo — AI writes your OLX listing and publishes it in one tap.

SellSnap is a Kotlin Multiplatform app (Compose Multiplatform) for people who sell second-hand
items on OLX in Ukraine, Poland, Portugal, Romania and Bulgaria.

- [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sirelon.sellsnap)
- [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6776001373)
- [Website](https://sirelon.github.io/SellSnap/) — served from [`docs/`](docs/)
