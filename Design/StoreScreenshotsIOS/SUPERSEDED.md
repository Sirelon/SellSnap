# Superseded

The generator that produced these iPhone JPGs was removed on 2026-08-03. It hard-coded
Ukrainian copy inline and paired each screenshot to its caption by sorted filename index,
so any re-capture silently shifted every caption by one.

Replaced by `Design/AppStoreScreenshots/` — multi-locale, and captions are bound to screens
by name via `scenes.json`. See its `PROGRESS.md` for the full rationale.

The JPGs here are the currently-shipped Ukrainian App Store assets (1284x2778, the now-
optional 6.5" slot) and are kept for reference only.
